I enjoy transforming complexity into clarity. My background combines UX/UI Design, Graphic Design, Front-end Development, Analytics and Stop-motion Animation.

Every project starts with understanding people — their goals, frustrations and decision-making process. I believe thoughtful interfaces should remove friction instead of adding more.

Outside of interface design I spent several years studying drawing and creating stop-motion animation, which shaped my visual thinking and attention to detail.
